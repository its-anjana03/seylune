# Seylune home — scene prototype

Scope: home page first, as a prototype for user approval; other pages follow only after the user approves it. Mode: Persuade / Experience.
Audience: portfolio visitors judging craft first, the imagined shopper second (see PRODUCT.md).
Constraints (user, 2026-10-21): original palette (night #151116, plum #221A24, deep #0F0D10, cream #F4EEE8, mist #B9A8AE, gold #C6A36B); no stacked scrolling page; liquid WebGL images; video or 3D opening; avoid every FORGE portfolio move (pinned rails, circle reveals, page wipes, smooth-scroll libraries, outline wordmark footer, serif-italic headlines).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A fashion film in six full-screen scenes: the house is shown one frame at a time, like a runway, never as a page. Refuses the stacked template (hero, card row, story block, footer) and the creative-studio scroll showcase.

OWN-WORLD: Night ground, plum as the lifted field, cream type, gold only for the crescent mark, the price and the active state. Type is wide uppercase sans (Tenor Sans) for scene titles with Manrope for reading text and UI; no italics, no serif display. Photographs live on WebGL planes that ripple under the cursor and melt into the next image.

STORY: The visitor watches the house open, browses pieces one at a time, chooses a collection, reads who Seylune is, sees the three edits, and leaves through a closing scene with the newsletter and links.

FIRST VIEWPORT (as built, after user feedback): the campaign photograph full-bleed on a liquid plane (the film was dropped at the user's request); the title, line and two buttons on the left; SEYLUNE set across the foot of the frame (running up the left edge on phones, sized to the space above the words). Corner header, no bar; no scene index and no announcement bar (both removed at the user's request).

FORM: Full-screen scene sequence, user-pinned after the roll (seed reference below). Signature: liquid displacement between photographs and a cursor ripple on every photograph plane; scene changes zoom-dissolve. seed: 70181dea68722700

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
